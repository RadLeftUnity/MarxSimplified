Lenin exposes how the Left Communists' high-sounding slogans of 'international revolutionary propaganda by deed' objectively play into the hands of imperialist provocateurs seeking to draw the young Soviet Republic into a premature, fatal military clash. He also clarifies the dialectical shift on 'defencism': while defending the fatherland in a bourgeois imperialist war is a betrayal of socialism, defending the socialist republic after the proletariat has seized state power is a sacred internationalist duty. Serious defense of the socialist fatherland requires iron proletarian discipline, accurate calculation of forces, and the willingness to make organized retreats when necessary.
