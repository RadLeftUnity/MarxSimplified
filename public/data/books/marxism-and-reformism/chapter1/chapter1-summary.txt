In this 1913 article published in Pravda Truda, V.I. Lenin analyzes the fundamental difference between Marxism and reformism. Unlike anarchists who reject fighting for immediate improvements, Marxists actively lead struggles for reforms that improve working conditions. However, Marxists wage an uncompromising battle against reformists who confine working-class struggle to partial reforms within the capitalist system.

Lenin exposes how the liberal bourgeoisie uses reforms as an instrument to pacify workers, divide them, and preserve wage-slavery. In contrast, conscious workers utilize reforms as tactical footholds to broaden and intensify the revolutionary class struggle. Lenin criticizes the Russian liquidators who abandoned the ultimate goal of working-class emancipation in favor of legalism, proving that independent class mobilization is the only genuine guarantee of lasting social gains.
