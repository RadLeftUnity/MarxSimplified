In this appendix, Lenin details the historical attempt to unite the Iskra group with the Rabocheye Dyelo organization, explaining why the unification failed due to unbridgeable tactical and theoretical disagreements.

Key Takeaways:
1. Tactical Disagreements: The attempt at unity demonstrated that organizational mergers are impossible when factions hold contradictory views on political agitation.
2. Principle Over Compromise: Unification without agreement on core principles produces unstable, short-lived alliances that collapse under pressure.
