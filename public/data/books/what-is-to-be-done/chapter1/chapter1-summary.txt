This opening preface explains why Lenin expanded his original pamphlet into a thorough critique of "Economism" (the opportunist trend advocating that workers focus purely on economic wage demands).

Key Takeaways:
1. Responding to Opportunism: Lenin outlines why a decisive polemic was necessary to clarify fundamental differences on strategy and organization.
2. Three Core Questions: The pamphlet addresses political agitation, organizational structure, and the plan for a nationwide political newspaper.
3. Real-life Example: If a labor movement only fights for minor wage increases while ignoring government censorship, police repression, and civil rights, it leaves the oppressive system intact.
