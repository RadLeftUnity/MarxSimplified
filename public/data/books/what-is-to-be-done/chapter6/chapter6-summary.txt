Lenin presents his famous proposal for an all-Russia newspaper (Iskra) as a "collective organizer" that serves as the scaffolding for a nationwide political party.

Key Takeaways:
1. Scaffolding for Organization: A shared nationwide publication does not just spread news; it coordinates local teams, establishes common messaging, and builds network infrastructure.
2. Collective Agitator and Organizer: Publishing and distributing a regular newspaper forces local groups to communicate, plan logistics, and operate as a unified body.
3. Real-life Example: Independent media outlets and digital news networks act as modern scaffolding, allowing geographically separated groups to coordinate actions around shared goals.
