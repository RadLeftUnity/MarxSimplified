Lenin analyzes the fashionable slogan "freedom of criticism," showing that it was used by opportunist reformists (like Eduard Bernstein) to dismantle core Marxist principles and turn revolutionary parties into mere reformist groups.

Key Takeaways:
1. Freedom of Criticism as Opportunism: In practice, "freedom of criticism" meant freedom to import bourgeois reformist ideas into the socialist movement.
2. The Need for Theory: Without revolutionary theory, there can be no revolutionary movement.
3. Real-life Example: A social movement that abandons clear theoretical principles easily gets co-opted by corporate sponsors and government promises of minor, non-structural tweaks.
