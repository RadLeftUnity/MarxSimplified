Lenin concludes the pamphlet by reflecting on the historical struggle against Economism and outlining the immediate steps required to build a united Social-Democratic Party in Russia.

Key Takeaways:
1. Overcoming Amateurism: The movement must move past fragmented, local methods to build a professional nationwide organization.
2. Ideological Demarcation: True unity can only be achieved by drawing clear lines of demarcation between revolutionary Social-Democracy and opportunist reformism.
