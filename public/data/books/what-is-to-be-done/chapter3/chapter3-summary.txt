Lenin examines the relationship between spontaneous worker uprisings and political consciousness. While spontaneous strikes show mass energy, they only develop into trade-union consciousness (demanding better wages) unless elevated by political education.

Key Takeaways:
1. Spontaneity vs. Consciousness: Spontaneous mass anger is the embryonic form of consciousness, but it needs theoretical guidance to become systematic political struggle.
2. Trade-Unionism vs. Socialism: Spontaneous worker struggles naturally limit themselves to bargaining within capitalism unless connected to a broader anti-capitalist vision.
3. Real-life Example: A spontaneous wildcat strike can win a temporary pay raise, but without political organization, management will eventually undermine those gains or retaliate when the anger cools.
