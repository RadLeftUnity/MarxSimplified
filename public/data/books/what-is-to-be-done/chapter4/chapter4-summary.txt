Lenin contrasts narrow trade-unionist politics (focusing solely on factory issues) with comprehensive political agitation that addresses all forms of oppression in society.

Key Takeaways:
1. Tribune of the People: Organizers must not act like narrow trade-union secretaries who only care about workplace disputes; they must be tribunes of the people who expose every act of government and police tyranny.
2. All-Sided Agitation: Socialists must champion democratic rights for all oppressed groups in society, not just economic demands for factory workers.
3. Real-life Example: A union that speaks out only when its own members' pay is cut, but remains silent on racism, war, or environmental destruction, fails to build real social solidarity.
