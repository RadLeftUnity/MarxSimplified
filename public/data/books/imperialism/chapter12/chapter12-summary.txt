Summary of Chapter: IX. Critique of Imperialism

Lenin evaluates various critiques of imperialism, distinguishing revolutionary Marxist criticism from reformist and petty-bourgeois critiques. He refutes ideas that imperialism can be softened through international courts, disarmament treaties, or free-trade agreements. Because economic growth under capitalism is inherently uneven, international alliances and cartels are merely temporary truces between wars over market re-division.
