Summary of Chapter: VII. Imperialism as a Special Stage of Capitalism

Lenin synthesizes his analysis into a precise five-point definition of imperialism as the monopoly stage of capitalism. He critiques Karl Kautsky's definition of imperialism as merely a 'preferred policy' of industrial nations, arguing instead that imperialism is an unavoidable, structural phase of capitalist development. He also refutes Kautsky's theory of 'ultra-imperialism', which claimed capitalists could peacefully manage the world.
