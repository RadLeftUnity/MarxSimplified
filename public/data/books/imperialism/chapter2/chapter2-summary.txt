Summary of Chapter: Preface (1917)

In the original 1917 Preface written in Zurich, Lenin explains that the pamphlet was composed under the constraints of tsarist censorship. As a result, he was forced to restrict his analysis strictly to theoretical and economic data, adopting an allegorical or 'Aesopian' language to bypass censors. He emphasizes that understanding the economic essence of imperialism is essential for evaluating modern war and global politics.
