Summary of Chapter: VIII. Parasitism and Decay of Capitalism

Lenin explores the parasitic tendencies inherent in monopoly capitalism. Monopolies often suppress technological innovations to protect existing capital investments. The economy becomes dominated by 'rentiers' (investors who live solely off coupon-clipping and dividends) and 'rentier states' that extract wealth from foreign countries. This parasitic wealth allows elites to bribe a domestic 'labour aristocracy', splitting the working-class movement.
