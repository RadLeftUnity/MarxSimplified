Summary of Chapter: X. The Place of Imperialism in History

Lenin concludes by defining imperialism's place in history as 'monopoly capitalism', 'parasitic capitalism', and 'moribund (dying) capitalism'. By thoroughly socialising production, organizing supply chains, and centralizing banking, monopoly capitalism builds the technical apparatus for a planned socialist economy. However, because private ownership remains, social contradictions are brought to their extreme tension, marking imperialism as the eve of socialist revolution.
