Summary of Chapter: Preface to the French and German Editions (1920)

Writing after WWI and the Russian Revolution, Lenin highlights that the war was predatory and imperialist on all sides: a conflict to partition colonies and financial spheres of influence. He outlines key economic trends: the division of world infrastructure (such as railways), the rise of superprofits, and the creation of a 'labour aristocracy' in wealthy nations bribed by colonial exploitation to support bourgeois politics.
