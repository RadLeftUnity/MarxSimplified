Sa'b details the institutional reality of the Israeli state, showing how its foundational racial ideology inevitably produces systemic discrimination. He documents the military government, land confiscations, and civil subjugation imposed upon indigenous Palestinian Arabs inside Israel. Furthermore, Sa'b exposes internal Israeli ethnic hierarchies, detailing the systemic socioeconomic and cultural marginalization inflicted by the dominant European Ashkenazi establishment upon Sephardic and Arab (Mizrahi) Jews.
