This chapter highlights the extensive historical tradition of Jewish dissent against political Zionism, citing prominent Jewish thinkers, philosophers, and religious leaders such as Albert Einstein, Morris Raphael Cohen, Lessing J. Rosenwald, and Judah Magnes. Sa'b shows that these thinkers vehemently rejected political Zionism's tribal chauvinism, warning that founding a sectarian state upon racial exclusivism and indigenous dispossession constituted a profound betrayal of Judaism's universal ethical message.
