In the concluding epilogue, Sa'b summarizes his indictment of political Zionism, defining it as an alien introduction of European racialism into the Middle East that subverts the region's historic monotheistic ideals of universal justice and human dignity. Quoting Princeton philosopher W. T. Stace and the 1964 Cairo Non-Aligned Movement resolution, Sa'b calls for the complete decolonization of Palestine and the restoration of inalienable Palestinian national rights in a democratic, anti-racial society.
