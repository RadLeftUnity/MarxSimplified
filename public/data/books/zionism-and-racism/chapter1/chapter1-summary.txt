Dr. Hasan Sa'b analyzes the philosophical and ideological foundations of political Zionism, arguing that it is fundamentally built upon the 19th-century European myth of a distinct, unassimilable 'Jewish race' with exclusive national rights. Examining early Zionist thinkers like Moses Hess and Theodor Herzl, Sa'b shows how Zionism rejected the universalist, assimilationist, and humanitarian ideals of modern democracy in favor of romantic racial exclusivism and settler-colonial statehood in Palestine.
