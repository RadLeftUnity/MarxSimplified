Summary of Women's Suffrage and Class Struggle.

In this famous 1912 speech delivered in Stuttgart, Rosa Luxemburg demonstrates that working-class women's demand for equal voting rights is a revolutionary threat to the capitalist state. Luxemburg contrasts working women—who create surplus value in factories, farms, and offices—with bourgeois women who act as co-consumers of surplus value, proving that female suffrage is an essential weapon in the proletarian class struggle.
