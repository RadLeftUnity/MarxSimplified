Che Guevara explains the organic, scientific character of the Cuban Revolution's Marxism. Rather than adopting dogmatic formulas, Cuban revolutionaries discovered and fulfilled the objective laws of historical materialism through practical armed struggle and agrarian transformation in the Sierra Maestra. Che emphasizes that Marxism is not an esoteric sect but a fundamental social science: just as physicists naturally rely on Newton, revolutionaries naturally rely on Marx to interpret and transform society.
