Reflecting on his early career as a physician and his journeys across Latin America, Che Guevara argues that isolated medical treatments cannot cure diseases born of malnutrition, rural poverty, and capitalist exploitation. A physician can only practice truly revolutionary medicine when supported by a social revolution that dismantles oppressive property relations. Che challenges medical workers to abandon individualist careerism, embrace rural service among the peasantry, and transform healthcare into a preventative, community-centered social right.
