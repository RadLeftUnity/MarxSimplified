Mao Tse-tung

On Guerrilla Warfare

Appendix: Tables of Organization for Guerrilla Forces


TABLE 1: ORGANIZATION OF AN INDEPENDENT GUERRILLA COMPANY

Command and Leadership:
- Company Leader: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Political Officer: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Executive Officer: 1 Personnel (Pistol)

Company Headquarters:
- Message Section Chief: 1 Personnel (Signal: 1)
- Administrative Section Chief: 1 Personnel (Rifle)
- Public Relations: 3 Personnel (Rifle)
- Duty Personnel: 2 Personnel
- Barber: 1 Personnel
- Cooks: 10 Personnel

Medical Section:
- Section Chief: 1 Personnel
- Assistant: 1 Personnel
- First Aid and Nursing: 4 Personnel

Intelligence Section:
- Section Chief: 1 Personnel (Rifle)
- Intelligence Personnel: 9 Personnel (Rifle)

Combat Units:
- Platoon Leaders: 3 Personnel (Rifle)
- Squad Leaders: 9 Personnel (Rifle)
- Nine Squads (8 men each): 72 Personnel (Rifle)

Company Totals:
- Total Personnel: 122 Men
- Total Armament: 3 Pistols, 98 Rifles

Notes on Company Organization:
1. Each squad consists of 9 to 11 men. If men or arms are insufficient, the third platoon may be omitted or one squad organized as company headquarters.
2. The mobile propaganda unit consists of company members who carry out political work when not engaged in fighting.
3. If personnel is limited, medical personnel (2 or 3) may be attached directly to the administrative section.
4. If no barber is available, it is unimportant. If cooks are lacking, company members may be designated to prepare food.
5. Every combatant soldier should be armed with a rifle. If rifles are insufficient, shotguns, lances, and big swords can be used.
6. Maximum company strength is 180 men (12 squads of 11). Minimum company strength is 82 men (6 squads of 9).


TABLE 2: ORGANIZATION OF AN INDEPENDENT GUERRILLA BATTALION

Command and Leadership:
- Battalion Commander: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Political Officer: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Executive Officer: 1 Personnel (Pistol)

Battalion Headquarters Sections:
- Signal Section: 2 Personnel
- Administrative Section Chief: 1 Personnel (Rifle)
- Administrative Runner: 1 Personnel (Rifle)
- Public Relations: 10 Personnel (Rifle)
- Duty Personnel: 2 Personnel
- Barbers: 3 Personnel
- Supply Officer: 1 Personnel
- Cooks: 10 Personnel

Medical Section:
- Medical Officer: 1 Personnel
- Stretcher-Bearers: 6 Personnel
- Nursing Personnel: 4 Personnel

Intelligence Section:
- Section Chief: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Intelligence Personnel: 30 Personnel (Pistol)

Machine-Gun Section:
- Machine-Gun Crews: As Available

Headquarters Totals:
- Total Headquarters: 75 Personnel (34 Pistols, 12 Rifles)

Subordinate Units and Battalion Totals:
- Three Companies (122 men each): 366 Personnel (9 Pistols, 294 Rifles)
- Total Battalion Strength: 441 Personnel (43 Pistols, 306 Rifles)

Notes on Battalion Organization:
1. Total headquarters strength may vary from 46 to 110 personnel.
2. When a battalion expands to 4 companies, regimental organization should be adopted.
3. Light machine-gun squads have 5 to 7 men; heavy machine-gun squads have 7 to 9 men.
4. The intelligence section operates in 2 to 4 squads, including plain-clothes operatives and mounted squads if horses are available.
5. If stretcher-bearers are unavailable, cooks or local civilian volunteers should be utilized.
6. Each company must possess at least 25 rifles; remaining arms can be shotguns, lances, or big swords.


TABLE 3: ORGANIZATION OF AN INDEPENDENT GUERRILLA REGIMENT

Command and Leadership:
- Regimental Commander: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Political Officer: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Chief of Staff: 1 Personnel (Pistol)

Staff Sections:
- Operations Officer: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Operations Clerks: 15 Personnel
- Intelligence Officer: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Intelligence Personnel: 36 Personnel (Pistols)
- Public Relations Officer: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Public Relations Personnel: 36 Personnel (Rifles)
- Administrative Officer: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Administrative Clerks: 15 Personnel (Pistols)
- Administrative Runner: 1 Personnel

Transport Section:
- Chief of Transport: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Finance Officer: 1 Personnel
- Traffic Manager: 1 Personnel (Pistol)
- Supply Officer: 1 Personnel
- Drivers: 5 Personnel

Medical Section:
- Chief of Medical Section: 1 Personnel
- Doctors: 2 Personnel
- Nurses: 15 Personnel

Headquarters Totals:
- Total Regimental Headquarters: 137 Personnel (60 Pistols, 36 Rifles)

Subordinate Battalions and Regimental Totals:
- Three Battalions (441 men each): 1,323 Personnel (129 Pistols, 918 Rifles)
- Total Regimental Strength: 1,460 Personnel (189 Pistols, 954 Rifles)

Notes on Regimental Organization:
1. Refer to Tables 1 and 2 for company and battalion structural details.
2. Battalions and companies do not maintain separate transport sections.
3. Each regiment should be equipped with more than 100 hand revolvers or pistols.


TABLE 4: ORGANIZATION OF AN INDEPENDENT GUERRILLA BRIGADE (OR DIVISION)

Brigade Structure Summary:
- Formed by combining two to four independent guerrilla regiments alongside supporting artillery and technical units as available.
