It is only within the working class that active opposition to existing bourgeois sexual norms is taking shape. The ideals and code of sexual morality that the working class develops serve as a new weapon in its social struggle.

The "captivity" of women in the home, the way family interests are placed before all else, and the exercise of property rights by husband over wife are all being broken down by the basic principle of working-class ideology: comradely solidarity.

Suppose family affairs require a businessman to take his capital out of a firm; bourgeois morality says "the interests of the family come first." Compare this with the attitude of workers to a strikebreaker who defies his comrades to earn wages for his family: working-class morality dictates "the interests of the class come first."

The principle of comradeship is basic to proletarian ideology. It determines the developing proletarian morality, allowing man to be capable of freedom instead of being bound by a sense of property, capable of comradeship rather than inequality and submission.

A social group works out its ideology and sexual morality in the process of its struggle with hostile social forces. Only a code of sexual morality in harmony with the problems of the working class can serve as an important weapon in strengthening the working class's fighting position.
