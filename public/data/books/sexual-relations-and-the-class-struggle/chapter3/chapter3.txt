The "sexual crisis" is made worse by two characteristics of modern psychology: the idea of "possessing" the married partner, and the belief that the two sexes are unequal in worth.

Bourgeois morality, with its individualistic family based entirely on private property, has carefully cultivated the idea that one partner should completely "possess" the other. It has extended the concept of property rights to include the right to the other person's whole spiritual and emotional world.

The "inequality" of the sexes: the inequality of their rights, the unequal value of their physical and emotional experience: is the other circumstance that distorts the modern psyche. The double morality inherent in bourgeois society has poisoned the psyche of men and women.

In the eyes of society, the personality of a man can be easily separated from his actions in the sexual sphere. The personality of a woman is judged almost exclusively in terms of her sexual life. We are used to evaluating a woman not as an independent personality, but only as an appendage of a man.

Only a change in the economic role of woman, and her independent involvement in production, will bring about the weakening of these mistaken and hypocritical ideas.
