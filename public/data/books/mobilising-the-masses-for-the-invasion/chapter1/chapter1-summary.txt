In this urgent address to sugar workers shortly before the Bay of Pigs invasion, Che Guevara warns that North American imperialism cannot tolerate the example of a free Cuba in the Americas. Che argues that vacillation or retreat is fatal against an imperialist enemy, citing the tragic assassination of Patrice Lumumba in the Congo. He concludes that while international socialist solidarity is vital, the definitive guarantee of victory lies in the domestic unity, productive labour, and fighting spirit of the Cuban people.
