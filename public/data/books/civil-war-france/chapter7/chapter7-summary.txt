Section IV of The Civil War in France. Marx recounts the heroic defense and eventual bloody suppression of the Paris Commune by Versaillist troops aided by Bismarck's Prussian army. Marx exposes the savage hypocrisy of the bourgeoisie, who slaughtered tens of thousands of unarmed Communard men, women, and children to protect private property, immortalizing the Communards as pioneers of a new socialist society.
