In this classic 1943 text, Mao Zedong formulates the Marxist-Leninist method of leadership and the mass line: "from the masses, to the masses." Mao explains that in any revolutionary or organizational work, communists must employ two fundamental methods: combining the general call with particular guidance, and combining the leadership with the masses.

Mao demonstrates that issued directives remain empty bureaucratic slogans unless leaders personally investigate concrete conditions in specific subordinate units, gain practical experience, and test theoretical formulations. Furthermore, leading cadres must organize an active leading nucleus linked closely with the masses, uniting the advanced elements to win over the intermediate and backward layers. All correct leadership requires taking the scattered, unsystematic ideas of the masses, concentrating and systematizing them through Marxist study, returning them to the masses through political agitation and education, and testing their correctness in mass struggle in an endless upward spiral.
