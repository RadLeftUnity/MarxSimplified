Lenin critiques Menshevik and Second International leaders (exemplified by N. Sukhanov) for their mechanical, fatalistic view of Marxism. The reformists dogmatically argued that Russia's productive forces had not matured enough for socialism. Lenin retorts that while general historical laws exist, imperialist world war created unique revolutionary conditions where the Russian working class and peasantry had to seize power first to achieve the cultural and industrial prerequisites of modern civilization.
