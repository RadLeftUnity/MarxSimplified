Lenin continues his rebuttal of Second International dogmatism, addressing the Menshevik argument that Russia lacked the cultural 'civilization' necessary for socialism. Citing Napoleon's dictum 'First engage in a serious battle and then see what happens', Lenin argues that seizing power in October 1917 allowed the Soviet state to expel landlords and capitalists, clearing the path to build socialist culture and industrialization far more rapidly than passive subservience to bourgeois development.
