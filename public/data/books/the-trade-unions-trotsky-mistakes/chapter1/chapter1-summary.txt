Lenin defines the dialectical role of trade unions under the dictatorship of the proletariat, arguing against Trotsky's attempt to administratively absorb the unions into the state apparatus. Lenin emphasizes that trade unions must function as a school of administration, management, and communism, relying on broad persuasion rather than military-administrative coercion, and serving as a vital link connecting the party vanguard to the working masses while protecting workers from bureaucratic distortions.
