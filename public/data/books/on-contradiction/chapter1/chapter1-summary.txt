This opening section introduces the central idea of the book: everything changes because of internal opposing forces pushing against each other (the law of contradiction).

Key Takeaways:
1. Opposing Forces Drive Change: Nothing stays completely static; progress comes from internal tensions within things.
2. Practical Study over Dogma: Philosophy should eliminate dogmatism (blindly memorizing theories without applying them to real life).
3. Real-life Example: If a sports team is struggling, blindly copying another team's strategy without looking at your own players' unique strengths and weaknesses will not solve your problems.
