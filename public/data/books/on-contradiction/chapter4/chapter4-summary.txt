This section explains why every situation is unique and requires a tailored approach:

1. Unique Problems Need Specific Solutions: Different kinds of conflicts cannot be solved with the same tool. Military battles, economic hardship, and internal team disagreements each require different methods.

2. How We Learn: Knowledge moves in a circle. We study specific real-world cases to form general principles, and then apply those principles back to analyze new, specific situations.

3. Concrete Analysis: Marxism stresses looking at actual conditions on the ground. Blindly applying a formula that worked somewhere else without checking your local situation leads to failure.

Real-life Example: If a local restaurant is losing business, the owner must investigate whether the issue is food quality, pricing, or service, rather than blindly copying an online marketing campaign designed for a clothing store.
