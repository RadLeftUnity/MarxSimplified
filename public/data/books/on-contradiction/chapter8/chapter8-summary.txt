This concluding section summarizes the entire philosophy and its practical purpose:

1. Universal Truth: Everything in nature, society, and human thought moves forward through the struggle of opposing forces.

2. Clear Priorities: Success comes from analyzing your specific situation, identifying the main problem (principal contradiction), and recognizing which force is growing.

3. Action over Dogma: Philosophy is only useful if it helps you understand real conditions and solve practical problems in the real world.

Real-life Example: Whether you are trying to resolve a personal challenge, run a team, or understand global news, don't rely on rigid rules. Identify the opposing forces at play, find the main roadblock, and focus your energy on solving that central challenge first.
