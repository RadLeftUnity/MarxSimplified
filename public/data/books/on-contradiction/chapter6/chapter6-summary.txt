This chapter explores how opposing sides are connected and how balance breaks down:

1. Opposites Need Each Other (Identity): Opposing sides define each other (like employer and employee, or seller and buyer). Under specific conditions, they can even trade places.

2. Peace Is Temporary, Conflict Is Constant: Periods of balance are temporary and depend on specific conditions. The underlying push and pull between opposing forces is continuous.

3. How Change Happens: The combination of temporary stability and continuous internal tension is what drives everything in nature and society to evolve.

Real-life Example: A lease agreement creates a temporary balance between a landlord and a tenant. But as market rents change or maintenance issues arise, underlying economic tensions surface, eventually leading to a new contract or moving out.
