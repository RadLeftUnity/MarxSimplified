In this pivotal chapter, Lenin elucidates the transition from capitalism to communism based on Marx's Critique of the Gotha Programme. He distinguishes between the lower phase of communism (socialism), where bourgeois right still regulates distribution according to labor performed, and the higher phase of communism, where society inscribes on its banners: "From each according to their ability, to each according to their needs," making any political coercive state completely superfluous.
