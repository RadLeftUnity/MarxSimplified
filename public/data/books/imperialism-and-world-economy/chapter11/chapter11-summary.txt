Bukharin critiques vulgar bourgeois and racial theories of imperialism (which falsely attribute modern wars to innate 'Slavic' or 'Teutonic' racial traits) as well as supra-historical definitions that equate modern imperialism with ancient Roman or Macedonian conquests. Using historical materialism, he defines imperialism as a specific historical category: the policy and ideology of finance capital aimed at reproducing and expanding state-capitalist monopoly relations on a world scale.
