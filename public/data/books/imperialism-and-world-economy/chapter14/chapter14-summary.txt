Bukharin subjects Karl Kautsky's theory of 'ultra-imperialism' to a rigorous materialist critique. While a single world trust is conceivable in pure abstraction, concrete economic analysis proves that international cartels only hold together when competing state-capitalist trusts possess roughly equal strength and have no hope of victory through struggle. Because capitalist development is inherently uneven, any comparative equality is constantly disrupted, making peaceful ultra-imperialism an opportunist utopia.
