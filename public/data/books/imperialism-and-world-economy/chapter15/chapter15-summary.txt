World War I acts as a gigantic crisis that accelerates the fundamental tendencies of monopoly capitalism. Bukharin documents how the war ruins small independent states, elevates the United States into the premier state-capitalist trust, and drives belligerent governments to establish state production monopolies, mixed enterprises, and centralized War Socialism (state-monopoly capitalism). Instead of softening contradictions, wartime state capitalism sharpens both inter-imperialist rivalry and domestic class exploitation.
