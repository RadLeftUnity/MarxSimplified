Within national boundaries, free competition is rapidly superseded by horizontal and vertical monopoly concentration (cartels, syndicates, combined enterprises) fused with giant banks into finance capital. Bukharin shows how the tariff policy of the bourgeoisie undergoes a complete transformation: protective tariffs cease to be defensive shields for weak infant industries and instead become aggressive weapons of cartel magnates, allowing monopolies to charge artificially high monopoly prices at home and use those superprofits to dump commodities below cost on the world market.
