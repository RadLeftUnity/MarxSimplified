Writing in November 1917 immediately after the victory of the October Socialist Revolution, Nikolai Bukharin recounts the turbulent two-year history of his manuscript, which was raided by tsarist military censors and later by Provisional Government cadets in July 1917. Composed while socialism was betrayed by Second International opportunists during World War I, the book now appears in print as revolutionary socialism triumphs in Russia and wages a direct struggle to relegate imperialism to the archives of history.
