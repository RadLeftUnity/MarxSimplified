In his December 1915 introduction to Bukharin's treatise, V.I. Lenin emphasizes that no scientific analysis of World War I is possible without examining imperialism as a definite historical stage in the development of monopoly capitalism. Lenin traces how nineteenth-century 'peaceful' competitive capitalism grew into twentieth-century finance capitalism dominated by monopoly trusts and a handful of financial oligarchs. He sharply refutes Karl Kautsky's opportunist theory of 'ultra-imperialism', demonstrating that the uneven, conflict-ridden development of national finance capitals will inevitably trigger revolutionary explosions long before a peaceful single world trust could ever form.
