Written by V.I. Lenin in May 1909 for Proletary following debates in the Russian Duma. Lenin outlines the Marxist dialectical materialist approach to religion, refuting both bourgeois anti-clericalism and anarchist phrase-mongering. He explains why religion is the opium of the people, rooted in the social helplessness of working masses under capitalism, and proves why the fight against religious prejudices must be subordinated to the concrete class struggle.
