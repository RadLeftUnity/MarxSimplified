Lenin analyzes the contrast between Frederick Engels's materialist critique of Eugen Dühring and the Machian critique offered by revisionists like Valentinov. While Engels attacked Dühring for inconsistencies, idealism, and eclecticism from the standpoint of consistent dialectical materialism, the Machians attacked Dühring precisely for his materialist propositions regarding the objective existence of nature and causality. The revisionists attack metaphysical materialism from the right, pushing philosophy toward subjective idealism.
