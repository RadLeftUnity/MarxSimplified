Lenin directly dissects the slogan matter has disappeared. He explains that modern physics had not disproved matter; it had merely discovered that the physical structure of matter is far more complex than previously imagined, moving from the atom to the subatomic electron. What has disappeared is not the objective reality of the physical world, but the metaphysical, mechanistic boundary that classical science assigned to matter. The electron is as inexhaustible as the atom, and dialectical materialism readily accommodates every new discovery concerning the changing physical states of matter.
