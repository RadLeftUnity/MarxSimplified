Lenin presents the ultimate scientific test that shatters empirio-criticism: the question of whether nature existed prior to humanity. Natural science, geology, paleontology, and astronomy conclusively prove that the earth existed for millions of years in an incandescent state where no living organisms, human beings, or sensory organs existed. Because Mach and Avenarius assert that the world is composed of sensations, they are forced into the absurd dilemma of either denying established natural science or admitting that an observing subject must have existed before life, which is pure religious creationism.
