Lenin opens his polemic by addressing the philosophical crisis within the Russian Marxist movement following the defeat of the 1905 Revolution. A faction of Bolshevik and Menshevik intellectuals, including Bogdanov, Bazarov, and Lunacharsky, published several volumes claiming that Marx's dialectical materialism was outdated and needed to be supplemented with the modern positivism of Ernst Mach and Richard Avenarius. Lenin demonstrates that their efforts represent not a creative development of Marxism, but an uncritical surrender to subjective idealism and agnosticism, which ultimately opens the door to fideism and religious mystification.
