Lenin elevates human social practice as the supreme and decisive criterion of truth in Marxist epistemology. Rooting his analysis in Marx's Theses on Feuerbach and Engels's writings, Lenin demonstrates that the success of human technology, industrial production, and scientific experiment provides definitive proof that our mental representations correspond to objective reality. While practice can never confirm or refute an idea once and for all, it is sufficiently definite to banish all forms of professorial skepticism and scholasticism.
