Lenin examines the theory of symbols or hieroglyphs popularized by the physicist Hermann von Helmholtz and mistakenly endorsed by Plekhanov. Helmholtz asserted that sensations are not images or copies of real objects, but conventional signs or hieroglyphs. Lenin strongly rejects the hieroglyphic theory: treating sensations as arbitrary symbols rather than objective reflections severs the dialectical link between consciousness and reality, opening the door to Kantian agnosticism. Sensations are true mental images, copies, and reflections of the external material world.
