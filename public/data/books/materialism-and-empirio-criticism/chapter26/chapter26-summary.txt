Lenin subjects Alexander Bogdanov's personal philosophical system, empirio-monism, to a detailed critique. Bogdanov claimed to overcome the dualism of Mach by declaring that the physical world is the socially organized experience of humanity, while the psychical world is individually organized experience. Lenin demonstrates that this formulation is pure subjective idealism: by defining physical nature as the product of human collective organization, Bogdanov stands historical materialism on its head, making material being dependent on social consciousness.
