Lenin addresses the fundamental question of partisanship in philosophy. He exposes the bourgeois myth of non-partisan, objective professorial philosophy. Behind the veil of academic neutrality, modern bourgeois professors act as learned flunkeys of the capitalist class, using Machism, pragmatism, and agnosticism to disarm the working class and defend theology. Philosophy has always been, and remains, an irreconcilable struggle between two fundamental parties: materialism and idealism, reflecting the objective class antagonisms of society.
