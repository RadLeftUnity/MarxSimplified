Lenin moves to the social sciences, examining the reactionary attempts by German empirio-criticists like Petzoldt and Blei to apply Machism to history and political economy. Blei attacked Marxist political economy as metaphysical because it recognizes objective economic laws, value, and surplus value. Lenin demonstrates that when applied to society, empirio-criticism completely obliterates historical materialism, reducing social relations to subjective psychological perceptions and justifying bourgeois class domination.
