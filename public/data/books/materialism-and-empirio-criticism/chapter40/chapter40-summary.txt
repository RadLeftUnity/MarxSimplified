Lenin dissects the concluding article in the revisionist anthology Studies in the Philosophy of Marxism, written by S. Suvorov. Suvorov attempted to build a social philosophy on biological energetics and physiological laws, substituting naturalistic jargon for class analysis and historical materialism. Lenin ridicules Suvorov's pseudo-scientific banalities, showing that replacing class struggle with abstract energetic formulas empties Marxism of all revolutionary and explanatory power.
