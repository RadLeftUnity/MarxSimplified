Lenin explores the close alliance between empirio-criticism and the German immanentist school represented by Schuppe, Leclair, Rehmke, and Schubert-Soldern. The immanentists openly declared that all reality is immanent in consciousness and utilized this doctrine to justify the existence of God, religious faith, and immortality. Lenin shows that Mach and Avenarius repeatedly praised the immanentists as close theoretical allies, proving that empirio-criticism inevitably acts as a philosophical handmaiden to religious fideism.
