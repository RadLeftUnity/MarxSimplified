Lenin critiques Mach's claim to have overcome both materialism and idealism through the discovery of neutral world-elements. Mach argued that colors, sounds, and pressures are physical when examined in relation to other external objects, and psychical when examined in relation to the human nervous system. Lenin exposes this formulation as sophistry: changing the word sensation to element does not alter the fact that Mach denies the objective existence of physical reality independent of human sensory faculties, reducing the universe to subjective perception.
