Lenin investigates why the German worker-philosopher Joseph Dietzgen was occasionally praised by reactionary bourgeois professors and cited by Russian Machians like Helfond. Dietzgen was an authentic materialist whose independent discovery of dialectical materialism was celebrated by Marx and Engels; however, Dietzgen occasionally used imprecise terminology, such as calling the universe a mental concept or broadening matter to include thought. Reactionaries seized upon these isolated slips to twist Dietzgen into an idealist ally, which Lenin vigorously refutes.
