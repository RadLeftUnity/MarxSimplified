Lenin critiques the Russian Machian Bazarov for accusing Plekhanov and orthodox Marxists of unlawful transcendence: meaning stepping beyond the boundaries of sensory experience by recognizing an objective material world outside human perception. Lenin shows that Bazarov is merely aping the agnosticism of David Hume and Neo-Kantianism. Engels specifically attacked agnosticism because it halts at sensations and refuses to recognize that sensations are caused by real material objects acting on our sensory organs.
