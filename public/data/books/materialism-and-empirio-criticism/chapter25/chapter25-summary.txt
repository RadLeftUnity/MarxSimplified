Lenin surveys the evolution of the Machian school following the deaths of its founders. He shows that the younger generation of empirio-criticists, such as Carstanjen and Cornelius, moved even more decisively toward open subjective idealism and fideism. Despite Bogdanov's desperate claim that Machism leaves no room for religion or God, contemporary bourgeois philosophy was actively employing Machian epistemology to defend clericalism and theological dogmas against scientific materialism.
