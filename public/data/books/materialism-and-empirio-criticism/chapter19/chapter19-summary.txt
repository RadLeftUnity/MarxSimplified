Lenin critiques Mach's famous principle of the economy of thought, which asserts that scientific concepts and laws are merely mental shortcuts adopted to describe sensations with minimal intellectual effort. Lenin demonstrates that taking economy of thought as an epistemological foundation is absurd: it is far more economical to assume the subjective idealist premise that only sensations exist, or even to invent God to explain everything in a single stroke. True scientific economy consists in discovering the real, objective material unity of the world.
