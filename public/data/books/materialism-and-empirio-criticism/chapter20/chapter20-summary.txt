Lenin investigates the nature of space and time. Dialectical materialism recognizes space and time as objective, mind-independent forms of the existence of matter in motion. Kantianism and Machism, by contrast, treat space and time as subjective human forms of intuition or sensory coordination. Drawing on biology and physics, Lenin demonstrates that modern science presupposes the objective reality of spatio-temporal dimensions, showing that space and time existed long before the emergence of human consciousness.
