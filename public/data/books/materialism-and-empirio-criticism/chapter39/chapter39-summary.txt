Lenin delivers a devastating critique of Alexander Bogdanov's sociological theory. In his work From the Psychology of Society, Bogdanov asserted that social being and social consciousness are identical. Lenin shows that this formula is an outrageous betrayal of historical materialism. Materialism proves that social being is primary and independent of social consciousness; humanity engages in economic production, exchange, and social relations long before developing conscious theoretical reflections of them. Bogdanov's identity of being and consciousness replaces historical materialism with pure subjective idealism.
