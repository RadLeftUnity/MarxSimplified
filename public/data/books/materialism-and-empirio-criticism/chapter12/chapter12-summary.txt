Lenin examines how Ludwig Feuerbach and worker-philosopher Joseph Dietzgen approached the thing-in-itself, refuting the Machian claim that materialists rejected the concept. Both Feuerbach and Dietzgen discarded only the Kantian agnostic mystification of the thing-in-itself as an unknowable realm beyond human experience, while fully upholding the materialist truth that things exist outside human consciousness and can be comprehended through sensory perception and scientific investigation.
