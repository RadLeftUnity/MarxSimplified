Writing in 1920 after the triumph of the Bolshevik Revolution and in the midst of the Russian Civil War, Lenin reaffirms the vital necessity of defending dialectical materialism against philosophical deviations. He highlights the continuing threat posed by Bogdanov's Machian conceptions, which were now being promoted under the slogan of Proletarian Culture through the Proletcult movement. Lenin explains that behind high-sounding revolutionary rhetoric, Bogdanov continued to advocate subjective-idealist errors that undermined the party's scientific, materialist foundation.
