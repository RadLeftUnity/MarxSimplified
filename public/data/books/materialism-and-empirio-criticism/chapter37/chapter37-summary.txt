Lenin synthesizes his analysis of the crisis in physics, defining the essence of physical idealism. The crisis arose because physicists were transitioning from old, metaphysical, mechanistic materialism to dialectical materialism. Encountering subatomic phenomena that could not be explained by mechanical rigidity, scientists untrained in dialectics fell into relativism, confusing the breakdown of old physical models with the disappearance of objective reality. Lenin declares that modern science is in labor, painfully giving birth to dialectical materialism.
