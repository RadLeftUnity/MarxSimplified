Lenin establishes the historical lineage of Machism by conducting a direct comparative analysis between modern empirio-criticism and the subjective idealism of Bishop George Berkeley from 1710. Berkeley openly declared that physical objects are merely collections of sensations, asserting that to exist is to be perceived. Lenin demonstrates that Berkeley's explicit purpose was to destroy the materialist concept of matter in order to preserve belief in God and combat atheism. By substituting the term elements for sensations, modern Machians merely disguise the fact that they are repeating Berkeley's theological idealism almost word for word.
