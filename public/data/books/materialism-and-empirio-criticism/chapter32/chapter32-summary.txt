Lenin critiques Wilhelm Ostwald's school of energetics and its influence on Alexander Bogdanov. Ostwald claimed that energy is the fundamental reality of the universe and that motion can be conceived without matter. Lenin demonstrates that energy and motion are meaningless abstractions when detached from an objective, moving material substrate. Divorcing motion from matter is simply an intellectual sleight of hand that transforms physical energy into a disembodied, spiritual force, which is idealism in disguise.
