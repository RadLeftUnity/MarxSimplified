Lenin scrutinizes the concepts of matter and experience as used by Mach, Avenarius, and their Russian followers. He shows that the Machians deliberately use the vague term experience to blur the boundary between the materialist line (experience is the reflection of the external world) and the idealist line (experience is self-contained consciousness). Lenin emphasizes that there are no philosophical definitions of matter other than the recognition that it is the objective reality given to humanity through sensory reflection.
