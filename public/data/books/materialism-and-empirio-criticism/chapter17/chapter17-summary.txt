Lenin briefly addresses an inaccuracy committed by Georgy Plekhanov in his introduction to Feuerbach. Plekhanov had suggested that for empirio-criticism, experience is merely an object of investigation rather than a means of knowledge. Lenin corrects this formulation, demonstrating that Avenarius and Mach do not simply treat experience as an object, but redefine experience subjectively to erase the distinction between sensation and the external physical world.
