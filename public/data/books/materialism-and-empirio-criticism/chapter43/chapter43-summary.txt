Lenin summarizes his entire treatise into four fundamental conclusions regarding empirio-criticism. First, its theoretical foundations are a direct repetition of Berkeleian subjective idealism. Second, its role in the revolution of modern physics is to promote physical idealism and agnosticism. Third, when applied to the social sciences, it completely undermines historical materialism and replaces it with subjective sociology. Fourth, behind its epistemological scholasticism, empirio-criticism represents the ideological struggle of reactionary parties against the scientific world-outlook of the revolutionary proletariat.
