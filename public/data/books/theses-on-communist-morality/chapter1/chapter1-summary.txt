Summary of Theses on Communist Morality in the Sphere of Marital Relations.

In this landmark 1921 text published in Kommunistka, Alexandra Kollontai outlines the transition from bourgeois individualist family contracts to communist relational ethics. She argues that as society socializes domestic labor, childcare, and healthcare, human relationships are freed from economic dependence, possessive jealousy, and financial coercion, forming a new morality based on mutual affection, freedom, and collective solidarity.
