In this November 1905 article published in Novaya Zhizn, V.I. Lenin strongly endorses the decision of the Executive Committee of the St. Petersburg Soviet of Workers' Deputies to deny representation to anarchists. Lenin clarifies that the Soviet is not a broad labor parliament or an organ of vague municipal self-government, but a militant fighting alliance of socialist and revolutionary democratic forces formed to wage open political struggle against tsarist absolutism.

Lenin establishes that a profound gulf separates scientific socialism from anarchism. Anarchist philosophy is bourgeois individualism turned inside out: rather than reflecting the socialized production of modern industrial capitalism and pointing toward a collective socialist future, anarchist ideas express the despair of the isolated small producer buffeted by blind market forces. By repudiating political struggle, anarchists disarm the proletariat, fracturing working-class solidarity and leaving workers politically defenseless against bourgeois manipulation. Lenin calls on the socialist party to intensify its political education and ideological combat to preserve working-class unity.
