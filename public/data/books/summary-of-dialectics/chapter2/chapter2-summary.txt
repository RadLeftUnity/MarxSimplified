Written in Bern in 1915, this celebrated philosophical fragment investigates the core of dialectics: 'the splitting of a single whole and the cognition of its contradictory parts'. Lenin contrasts the two fundamental conceptions of development: the vulgar evolutionary conception of mechanical increase or decrease, versus the dialectical conception of self-movement driven by the struggle of internal opposites. He analyzes the relative nature of unity and the absolute nature of struggle, concluding with a profound epistemological analysis of how philosophical idealism functions as a 'sterile flower' rooted in the one-sided exaggeration of human knowledge.
