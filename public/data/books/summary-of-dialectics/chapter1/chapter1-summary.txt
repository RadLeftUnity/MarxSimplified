Written in 1914 as part of Lenin's conspectus of Hegel's Science of Logic in his Philosophical Notebooks, this fragment distills the fundamental elements of dialectical materialism. Lenin defines dialectics in brief as 'the doctrine of the unity of opposites' and outlines sixteen precise elements of dialectics, spanning the objective consideration of things in their development, the struggle and transitions of internal contradictions, the deepening of knowledge from appearance to essence, and the negation of the negation.
