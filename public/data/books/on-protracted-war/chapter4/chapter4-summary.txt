Mao refutes the defeatist "theory of national subjugation" by analyzing the historical characteristics of the 1930s epoch. Unlike historical conquests or Britain's colonization of India during the ascendancy of British capitalism, Japan is a moribund imperialist power launching an adventurist war on the eve of the general collapse of world imperialism. Conversely, China is in an unprecedented era of historical progress: it possesses a modern proletariat, an awakened peasantry, the Communist Party of China, the politically conscious Eighth Route Army, seventeen years of revolutionary experience, and the international support of the socialist Soviet Union and world popular movements.
