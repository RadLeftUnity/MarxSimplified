Addressing honest patriots worried about elite capitulation and Kuomintang corruption, Mao demonstrates why compromise with Japan cannot succeed and why domestic political progress is inevitable. Because Japanese imperialism pursues total material plunder and spiritual enslavement, absolute hostility has crystallized across China; combined with the firm resistance led by the Communist Party and support from the Soviet Union, the majority will defeat capitulationist schemes. Furthermore, Mao explains that a just revolutionary war acts as an antitoxin that not only defeats the foreign aggressor but also purges domestic corruption and transforms both China and Japan.
