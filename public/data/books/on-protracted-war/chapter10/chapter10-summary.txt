Mao situates China's War of Resistance within the global struggle against fascism and imperialism for perpetual human peace. Distinguishing between unjust wars that impede social progress (such as World War I or fascist aggression) and just revolutionary wars of liberation, he argues that the general crisis of world capitalism is driving fascist powers into war adventures that will hasten capitalism's collapse. Once humanity eliminates capitalism and imperialism, classes and war will be abolished forever.
